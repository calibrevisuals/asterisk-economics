How Is Boeing Still in Business After All This?

Boeing from the 737 Max grounding in 2019 to its third quarter of 2026: the losses, the two-maker jet market, what Washington does for Boeing, and how it raised money in 2020 and 2024

Watch the video. Every fact in this episode comes from the sources below.

Primary sources

  • Boeing, third-quarter 2026 results, SEC 8-K exhibit 99.1 (27 Oct 2026)Over 6,300 airplanes on order worth a record $612 billion; the quarter's $116 million loss; debt of $45.9 billion and $20.7 billion in cash and securities.

  • Boeing annual report (Form 10-K) for 2025About 182,000 employees at the end of 2025, and the line that 737 production can only rise with the FAA's agreement.

  • Boeing annual report (Form 10-K) for 2024, statement of operationsThe yearly losses that add up to about $35.7 billion from 2019 to 2024.

  • Federal Reserve press release: new facilities to support the flow of credit (23 Mar 2020)The Fed's promise to buy corporate bonds, made before Boeing's bond sale.

  • FAA emergency order grounding the 737 Max, Federal Register (18 Mar 2019)The March 2019 US grounding that opens the video.

  • NTSB final report AIR-25-04 on Alaska Airlines flight 1282 (2025)The door plug that blew out in January 2024.
