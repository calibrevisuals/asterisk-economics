How Is Congress Still Allowed to Trade Stocks?

Why members of Congress can still buy and sell stocks: the 2012 STOCK Act and its 45-day disclosure form, one real 2022 trade read start to finish, the 2020 Senate probes, and every ban since, up to the 53-47 Senate vote on 30 September 2026

Every fact in this episode comes from the sources below.

Primary sources

  • House Periodic Transaction Report #20021374 (filed 14 Jul 2022), Office of the ClerkThe form in the hook: owner SP (spouse), 20,000 Nvidia shares bought on 17 Jun 2022 by exercising call options at $100, amount $1,000,001 to $5,000,000.

  • House Periodic Transaction Report #20021438 (filed 26 Jul 2022), Office of the Clerk25,000 Nvidia shares sold on 26 Jul 2022 at an average of $165.05, for a stated loss of $341,365.

  • STOCK Act, Public Law 112-105 (signed 4 Apr 2012), govinfoReports due within 30 days of notice and never later than 45 days after the trade; insider trading laws apply to members and staff.

  • House Committee on Ethics, "PTR Due Date" guidance (Jan 2023)The 30-day grace period and the $200 minimum fee for each late transaction.

  • House Clerk, Roll Call 280, Stop Insider Trading Act (22 Jul 2026)The House vote, 232 to 198.

  • Public disclosure search: House (disclosures-clerk.house.gov) and Senate (efdsearch.senate.gov)Where anyone can look up the forms for free.
