OpenAI's $500 Billion Stargate Gamble Is Already Shrinking

What is left of the $500 billion Stargate promise: the four launch-day numbers checked one by one, how much money was really lined up, how much power was switched on, how OpenAI now counts rented computing as Stargate, who pays for the buildings (Oracle and its lenders), the New Mexico site still waiting for power and a permit, and the jobs

Every fact in this episode comes from the sources below.

Primary sources

  • OpenAI, "Announcing The Stargate Project" (21 Jan 2025)The launch-day numbers: $500 billion over four years, $100 billion right away, the four partners, and hundreds of thousands of American jobs.

  • Epoch AI, "OpenAI Stargate: where the US sites stand" (17 Apr 2026)Satellite check of the seven US sites: about 9.6 gigawatts planned, about 0.3 gigawatts (about 3%) running, all of it in Abilene.

  • CNBC, "OpenAI resets spend expectations, targets around $600 billion by 2030" (20 Feb 2026)OpenAI told investors it planned to spend about $600 billion on computing through 2030, down from about $1.4 trillion in deals talked about in late 2025.

  • DatacenterDynamics, "OpenAI claims to have secured 10GW of AI infrastructure capacity ahead of 2029 target" (30 Apr 2026)The 10 gigawatts OpenAI says it has secured, which includes computing it rents.

  • Bloomberg (via Bloomberg Law), "Stargate's first data center located in Texas with some 57 jobs" (24 Jan 2025)The first filing for the Abilene site listed at least 57 full-time jobs.

  • The Next Web, Oracle's $16 billion Michigan data-center financing (25 Apr 2026)US banks pulled back, PIMCO took about $10 billion at 7.5% for about 20 years; loans on Oracle's partner sites total about $72 billion.

  • Searchlight New Mexico, "State, developers ask to delay public hearing on Project Jupiter air quality permit" (5 Nov 2026)The developer gave the state more time, the December hearing was called off with no new date, and the governor-elect's plan for large data centers.

  • The Star (reporting the Financial Times), Oracle's $18 billion data-center debt under pressure (19 Sep 2026)Buyers offered the banks about 90 cents on the dollar for the New Mexico loan.

  • Yahoo Finance (24/7 Wall St.), a mystery seller puts AI data-center loans up for sealed bids (6 Nov 2026)A $25 million slice of the New Mexico loan up for sealed bids, due 19 November.

  • Yahoo Finance (24/7 Wall St.), OpenAI says it will pay for its data centers' power (6 Nov 2026)OpenAI's voluntary pledge to pay for power and grid upgrades at campuses it owns and runs; 40 of 41 local votes on data centers went against them.
