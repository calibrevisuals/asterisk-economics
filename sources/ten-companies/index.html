These 10 Companies Are Actually Holding Up America

How much of America now leans on its ten biggest companies: their weight in the S&P 500 (as much as the smallest 460 put together), the 1932 record, the cloud, apps and data-center building they carry, the world that leans on them too, why seven of them are one AI bet, and what their own filings say could go wrong

Every fact in this episode comes from the sources below.

Primary sources

  • State Street, SPDR S&P 500 ETF Trust (SPY) daily holdings file, as of 5 Nov 2026The weights of the ten biggest companies (39.08% together) and of the smallest ~460 holdings (39.35% together), measured by us from the file after the 5 Nov sell-off.

  • Morningstar, "US stock market concentration has surpassed its 1930s peak" (Dan Lefkovitz, 26 Feb 2026), using CRSP dataTop 10 = 37.7% of the whole US market on 31 Oct 2025, above the old month-end record of 37.3% on 31 May 1932.

  • Nvidia, Form 8-K press release, second quarter fiscal 2027 results (26 Aug 2026), SEC EDGARRevenue of $96.2 billion in one quarter; data center revenue $89.0 billion.

  • Nvidia, Form 10-Q for the quarter to 26 Jul 2026, SEC EDGAROne direct customer was 16% of revenue; revenue billed to customers based in Taiwan about $27.0 billion in the quarter.

  • Annual reports (Form 10-K) and latest quarterly reports of Apple, Nvidia, Microsoft, Alphabet, Amazon, Broadcom, Meta, Eli Lilly, Berkshire Hathaway and Tesla, SEC EDGARRevenue, profit, research spending, capital spending, income tax paid and employees in each company's latest full year; the risk sections (Nvidia: supply "especially in Taiwan and South Korea"; Apple: manufacturing by partners in Asia); Meta's 3.64 billion daily active people (September 2026).

  • Synergy Research Group, Q2 2026 cloud market (30 Jul 2026)Amazon 28%, Microsoft 20% and Google 15% of worldwide cloud infrastructure: 63% together.

  • US Bureau of Economic Analysis, GDP advance estimate, third quarter 2026 (29 Oct 2026)Growth at a 3.4% annual rate; equipment spending led by information processing equipment.
