What Happens to Nvidia Without Jensen Huang?

Jensen Huang's resignation from Nvidia after 33 years: what actually happened over four days (Megaspeed, the New York Times report, his email, the second relationship, the board), who runs Nvidia now, why one man mattered so much, and what happened at Intel, McDonald's, Apple and Microsoft when they lost the person at the top

Every fact in this episode comes from the sources below.

Primary sources

  • Nvidia, "NVIDIA Announces CEO Transition" (12 Nov 2026)Huang "has resigned as President, Chief Executive Officer, and a member of the Board, effective immediately"; Colette Kress interim CEO and still CFO; Harvey C. Jones first-ever board chair; earnings on 17 Nov as scheduled.

  • Nvidia, Form 8-K filed with the SEC (11 Nov 2026)The board accepted his resignation on Wednesday, 11 Nov, a day before it was announced.

  • Nvidia, "A Message to Our Company" (8 Nov 2026)Huang's email to every employee: "I was unfaithful to my wife... There is no excuse for what I did. I am deeply sorry."

  • The New York Times, report on Megaspeed and US officials' concerns (29 Oct 2026)US officials concluded a Megaspeed executive was working for Chinese intelligence and had gotten close to Huang. An allegation: nobody has been charged, and Megaspeed says it follows all laws.

  • Fortune, on the board, the spy allegations and the affairs (11 Nov 2026)The Wall Street Journal and New York Times reports of 11 Nov, CBS's count of more than $450 billion lost since 8 Nov, and Huang calling and texting clients himself.
