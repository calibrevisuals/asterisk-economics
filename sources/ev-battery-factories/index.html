What Happened to America's Giant EV Battery Factories?

What happened to the giant EV battery plants announced across America from 2021 to 2023: why they all got built at once, what the factory tax credit pays for, why car buyers came slower than planned, the plants that stopped (Big Rapids, Michigan and Glendale, Kentucky), the many that switched to batteries for the power grid and data centers, why switching works, and what happened to the jobs and the taxpayer deals

Every fact in this episode comes from the sources below.

Primary sources

  • Reuters, report on EV and battery factory cancellations with Atlas Public Policy data (19 Sep 2026, via Ya Libnan)About 55 billion dollars of new EV and battery factories announced in 2023 and about 6.5 billion in 2025, close to 20 billion dollars of projects cancelled in 2025, about 27,000 promised jobs on cancelled projects, Glendale's size, and about 2,100 hires planned from late 2027.

  • Spectrum News, BlueOval SK split and Kentucky workers (16 Dec 2025)The CEO's video telling staff all BlueOval SK positions in Kentucky would be eliminated, and how many of the nearly 5,000 promised jobs had been filled.

  • Lexington Herald-Leader, Ford and SK On to repay Kentucky's 250 million dollar loan (via AOL)Peak staff of about 1,850, about 1,600 jobs cut, the second Kentucky plant never making a battery, and loan repayments from March 2027.

  • Electrek, Ford pivots EV battery plants to grid and data-center storage (15 Dec 2025)Ford's plan to put about 2 billion dollars into the first Glendale plant for energy-storage batteries by late 2027.

  • Bridge Michigan, lawmakers on the Gotion deal (29 Oct 2025)Gotion's 2022 plan for Big Rapids and 2,350 jobs, the 23.7 million dollars for land, the state seeking 23.5 million back, a lawmaker's estimate that the land is worth about 6 million, and Gotion's view that it didn't walk away.

  • Battery Technology, GM exits Samsung SDI venture as the Ohio Ultium plant restarts (13 Aug 2026)Lordstown's restart on storage cells, Samsung SDI taking New Carlisle, and the cost of retooling a line.

  • HPPR / KMUW, Panasonic to shift some Kansas lines to AI data-center batteries (18 Jun 2026)De Soto's size, staff, projected 4,000 jobs, nearly 830 million dollars in Kansas tax credits, and the switch to data-center cells.

  • Miller & Chevalier, the 2025 tax law and the 45X factory credit (6 Aug 2025)The factory credit (35 dollars per kWh of cells, 10 per kWh of modules) kept by the 2025 law, its phase-down after 2029 and the new rules on Chinese suppliers.

  • GCN, South Korean battery makers begin grid production in the US (3 Oct 2026)The end of the 7,500 dollar buyer credit on 30 Sep 2025, grid-battery growth in 2026, and Kokomo and Ohio plants starting storage cells.
