Something Weird Keeps Happening Minutes Before the Market Moves

Oil bets reported as suspicious before Iran war news (March to May 2026), and the small insider cases that did get caught

Watch the video. Every fact in this episode comes from the sources below.

Primary sources

  • CFTC press release 9217-26: charges against a U.S. service member over Maduro-related event contracts (23 Apr 2026)The first time the CFTC used the "Eddie Murphy rule" to bring charges, 16 years after it was written. The soldier is accused, not convicted.

  • Dodd-Frank Wall Street Reform and Consumer Protection Act (Public Law 111-203, 2010), Section 746The "Eddie Murphy rule": no trading on non-public information taken from a government source.

  • Rep. Ritchie Torres asks the CFTC to investigate a $760 million oil futures trade before Iran's Strait of Hormuz announcement (April 2026)Round 3 of the five oil rounds.

  • Sen. Raphael Warnock and colleagues push the CFTC for a public update on suspicious oil futures trades (27 May 2026)Round 5: more than $700 million in bets in the early hours of 6 May, about an hour before the news.

  • Augustin, Brenner and Subrahmanyam, "Informed Options Trading Prior to Takeover Announcements: Insider Trading?", Management Science (2019)About 1 in 4 U.S. takeover deals shows unusual options trading before the announcement. Pattern evidence, not proof about any single deal.
